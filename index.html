About & Research

My mathematical research centers on Harmonic Analysis and operator theory on function spaces with non-standard growth. In particular, my doctoral thesis investigates the boundedness of the Hardy–Littlewood maximal operator $\mathcal{M}$ on variable Lebesgue spaces $L^{p(\cdot)}(X)$ defined over spaces of homogeneous type $(X, d, \mu)$.

Alongside pure analytical theory, I am deeply interested in interdisciplinary mathematical modeling and quantitative R&D applications in medicine.

Maximal & Singular Operators
