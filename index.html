01 / Profile

Research Focus

My research lies in Harmonic Analysis and Operator Theory, with a primary focus on analysis in non-Euclidean settings and function spaces with non-standard growth. My doctoral thesis investigates the Hardy–Littlewood maximal operator $M$ on variable Lebesgue spaces $L^{p(\cdot)}$, Musielak--Orlicz spaces $L^\varphi$, and quasi-Banach lattices over spaces of homogeneous type $(\Omega, d, \mu)$.

Alongside pure analytical theory, I am deeply interested in interdisciplinary mathematical modeling and quantitative R&D applications in medicine.

$\mathcal{M}f$

Maximal & Singular Operators

Boundedness criteria, log-Hölder continuity conditions, and weighted inequalities for operators in harmonic analysis.

$L^{p(\cdot)}$

Spaces of Homogeneous Type

Analysis on metric measure spaces $(X, d, \mu)$ equipped with a quasi-metric and a doubling measure.
